# Animation Design Skill - Búfalo Studio

## Motion Philosophy
Motion in Búfalo Studio must feel "expensive", disciplined, and smooth—reflecting high engineering precision and luxury aesthetic.

## Patterns
1. **Fade-Up Reveal**: Elements reveal as scroll enters threshold with `y: 30px`, `opacity: 0` -> `y: 0`, `opacity: 1` over `0.8s`.
2. **Background Color Shifts**: Transition from `#EDEDED` (Light Editorial) to `#171717` (Dark Studio) upon scrolling into Chapter 02 (Estética), and back to `#EDEDED` in Chapter 03 (Inteligencia).
3. **Data Science Canvas Animations**: Real-time graph drawing and statistical counters.
